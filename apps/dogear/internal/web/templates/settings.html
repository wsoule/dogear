Bring your saves from Pocket, Raindrop, your browser, or YouTube. Your tags and folders come along as tags.

{{template "csrf" $.CSRF}}

Off by default: new tags start private, so nothing from an old export shows on your profile until you choose. Your archive page shows saves that are in no tag or only in public tags.

where to get your export

Pocket
The CSV (or the zip holding it) from Pocket's export page, getpocket.com/export. Pocket tags become tags.
Raindrop
Settings → Export, as CSV or HTML. Collections and tags become tags; your notes come along.
Browser
Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge: bookmarks manager → export bookmarks to HTML. Folders become tags. Pinboard's HTML export works too.
YouTube
Google Takeout → YouTube and YouTube Music → playlists. Upload “Liked videos.csv”, another playlist's CSV, or the whole Takeout zip.

Links already on Dogear go straight into your archive. New ones are added just for you: anyone can see them in your archive and tags, but they stay out of feeds and search until someone posts them. Running the same file again adds nothing twice.

To save single links as you go, put Dogear in your phone's share menu: save from your phone.
