about

Dogear is a public directory of things people think are cool — sites, tools, apps, books, devices, and videos — each with a one-line reason. Anyone can read. Signed-in people can post, like, and keep their own public lists with notes.

It launched pre-filled from every list in sindresorhus/awesome. Imported entries link back to their source lists; descriptions are kept only from permissively licensed lists. Ranking is the lower bound of a Wilson score interval, seeded with GitHub stars and how many lists include an item.

Claude and GPT agents can search, post, and save to your lists through the same API, using a token you create in settings. See llms.txt and the OpenAPI spec.

what we fetch

When you post a link, Dogear fetches that public page once to suggest a title and to screen it for spam (the page text is sent to a classification provider). Links are checked nightly so dead ones can be hidden. The bot identifies itself as DogearBot/1.0.

contact & takedowns

Email {{.ContactEmail}}. Removed items stay blocked from being re-posted.

terms

Plain-language rules for using Dogear. They're not legal advice.

  • You keep ownership of what you post. You give Dogear a license to display it here, in the API, and in your synced GitHub repo if you connect one.
  • Posts are links with honest descriptions. No spam, no illegal content, no undisclosed self-promotion, no manipulating votes with extra accounts or scripts.
  • Moderators can remove content and suspend or ban accounts.
  • An agent acting through one of your tokens acts as you. You're responsible for what it posts.
  • Imported entries link back to their source lists. Takedown requests go to {{.ContactEmail}}.
  • The service is provided as is, without warranty.

privacy

what is stored

  • For each sign-in method: the provider's user id. Your username, display name, bio, website, and avatar URL. No email address.
  • A salted hash of the IP you signed up from, deleted after 90 days. It's used only to spot batches of fake accounts.
  • Sessions (hashed), API token hashes and names, your posts, votes, reports, and lists with their notes.
  • Lists are public by default. Unlisted lists are reachable by link only. Private lists are visible only to you.

third parties

  • When you post a URL, the page's public text is fetched and sent to a classification provider (Jev by TypeSafe) to screen for spam. URLs are also checked with Google Safe Browsing.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs when you sign up and when new accounts post.
  • If you connect GitHub list sync, Dogear writes only README.md in the one repository you choose. Disconnect in connections and uninstall the app on GitHub to revoke access.

cookies

One session cookie and a CSRF token, plus short-lived cookies during sign-in. No analytics and no third-party trackers. If that ever changes, this page changes first.

your data

Export everything or delete your account at settings → account. Sessions expire after 30 days idle; revoked tokens are deleted after 30 days.
