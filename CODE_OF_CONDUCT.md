# Code of Conduct

Dogear follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, and
make room for others. Harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language
are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **hello@dogear.fyi**. Reports are handled
privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block people who
break this code of conduct.
